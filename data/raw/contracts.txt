A contract under Indian law is an agreement enforceable by law.

Essential elements of a valid contract:
- Offer and acceptance
- Free consent
- Lawful consideration
- Capacity of parties
- Lawful object

Types of contracts:
- Valid contract
- Void contract
- Voidable contract

Important points:
- Breach of contract leads to legal consequences
- Damages may be claimed
- Contracts can be written or oral (in some cases)
