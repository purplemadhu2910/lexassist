A rental agreement is a legal contract between a landlord and tenant.

Key elements of a rental agreement:
- Names of landlord and tenant
- Property details
- Rent amount and payment terms
- Security deposit
- Duration of agreement
- Maintenance responsibilities
- Termination conditions

Legal points:
- Agreement should be in writing
- Stamp duty and registration may be required
- Tenant must follow terms to avoid eviction
- Landlord must return deposit as per agreement
