GST (Goods and Services Tax) registration is required for businesses in India under certain conditions.

Documents required for GST registration:
- PAN Card of business/entity
- Aadhaar Card
- Proof of business registration (if applicable)
- Address proof of business place
- Bank account statement or cancelled cheque
- Digital Signature (for companies and LLPs)

GST is mandatory if:
- Annual turnover exceeds prescribed limit
- Business involves interstate supply
- E-commerce selling is involved

GST helps in:
- Tax transparency
- Avoiding multiple taxes
- Legal compliance
