To open a business in India, the following documents are generally required:

- PAN Card of the owner or company
- Aadhaar Card for identity verification
- Address proof of business (electricity bill, rent agreement, etc.)
- Bank account details
- Passport-size photographs
- GST registration (if turnover exceeds threshold or applicable)
- Business registration certificate (for company/LLP)
- Digital Signature Certificate (for company registration)

Types of business structures in India:
- Sole Proprietorship
- Partnership
- Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- Private Limited Company
